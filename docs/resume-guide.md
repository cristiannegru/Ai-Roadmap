# 📄 AI Engineer Resume Guide

A highly optimized, metrics-driven resume is your key to getting callbacks for AI, ML, and Generative AI roles. Recruiters spend an average of 6 seconds reviewing a resume. This guide ensures your resume stands out.

---

## 🗺️ Resume Architecture
```
[Contact Info] ──> [Professional Summary] ──> [Skills Matrix] ──> [Work Experience (Metrics!)] ──> [AI Projects]
```

## 📌 1. The Structure of a Winning AI Resume

Keep your resume to **1 page** unless you have 8+ years of relevant industry experience. Use a clean, single-column, ATS-friendly format.

### Recommended Order:
1. **Header**: Name, Title (e.g., *AI Engineer* or *ML Engineer*), Email, Phone, LinkedIn, GitHub, Portfolio website.
2. **Skills Matrix**: Grouped by categorization (Languages, Frameworks, Generative AI/LLM, Cloud/Ops).
3. **Professional Experience**: Focus on what you built, how you built it, and the impact (metrics!).
4. **AI Projects**: Showcase 2-3 prominent open-source or deployment projects with GitHub links.
5. **Education**: Degree, University, Graduation Year.

## 📌 3. Writing Bullet Points with Impact (The XYZ Formula)
Never write what you did in a passive tone. Use Google's **XYZ Formula**:
> *"Accomplished [X], as measured by [Y], by doing [Z]."*

### ❌ Bad (Passive):
* "Worked on a RAG chatbot to answer company documents."
* "Trained machine learning models to improve prediction accuracy."

### ✅ Good (Metrics-driven & Active):
* "Designed and deployed a **RAG Chatbot** using LangChain and Pinecone, reducing customer support response latency by **40%** and handling 50k+ weekly active requests."
* "Fine-tuned **Llama 3** on custom medical datasets using QLoRA and Unsloth, improving diagnostic text relevance scores by **18%** while reducing model memory footprint by **65%**."
* "Optimized object-detection preprocessing pipelines in OpenCV, increasing video throughput from **22 FPS to 45 FPS**."

---

## 📌 4. Resume Action Verbs for AI Engineers
Use strong action verbs to start your bullet points:
* **Generative AI / LLM**: Deployed, Fine-tuned, Orchestrated, Engineered, Integrated, Prompted.
* **ML / DL**: Trained, Optimized, Feature-engineered, Programmed, Evaluated, Regularized.
* **Ops / Scale**: Containerized, Scaled, Streamlined, Automated, Deployed, Monitored.
